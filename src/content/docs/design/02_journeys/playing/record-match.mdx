## Goal
Record a match and add it to your Personal Gaming Archive.

## Why
A match is more than a winner and a score.
Recording a match creates connections between you, the game, and the people you played with —
connections that persist even for players who have not yet joined Stratastax.
Every match recorded now becomes part of a growing, permanent history.

## Outcome
A permanent, finalized match record exists, connected to the game, its Playset (if any), and all participants.
Each participant's Personal Gaming Archive is updated with this match, including their individual
result — score, rank, faction, or outcome.
Players without an account are represented as Ghost Players and can claim their history when they join.
