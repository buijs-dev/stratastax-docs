## Purpose

A Community gives a set of Users a shared identity and shared match
history — a family, a regular playgroup, a club. It is owned by exactly one
User, who may delegate admin rights to others by referencing one of their
own Groups as the Community's **AdminGroup**. This reuses Group entirely
as-is (see **Group**) — Community does not require any change to how Group
works, and inherits its non-escalation property for free: only the
Community's owner can change who is in the AdminGroup, because only a
Group's owner can change its members.

---

## Responsibilities

A Community is responsible for:

- having a Name and Description;
- being owned by exactly one User, with ownership that can be transferred;
- optionally referencing an AdminGroup — a Group owned by the Community's
current owner — whose members hold admin rights over the Community;
- defining its JoinPolicy (`Open` or `RequiresApproval`);
- holding Memberships, one per member User;
- being the target of MembershipRequests when its JoinPolicy is
`RequiresApproval`.

---

## Not Responsible For

A Community is NOT responsible for:

- managing its AdminGroup's member list — that belongs entirely to Group;
the Community only stores which Group, if any, is designated as its
AdminGroup;
- a member's Privacy Levels — joining a Community never changes what a
member's own PrivacySettings expose; a Community can only ever show what
a member has independently chosen to share;
- computing shared statistics or leaderboards — those are read from Match
and Membership data by something else, not owned by Community itself.

---

## Rights Model

- **Owner**: full rights. Only the owner may transfer ownership, set/change/
clear the AdminGroup, change the JoinPolicy, delete the Community, and
remove a member who currently holds admin rights — in addition to
everything an admin can do.
- **Admin** (owner, or any member of the AdminGroup, if set): may approve or
reject MembershipRequests, edit the Community's Name and Description, and
remove a non-admin member.
- **Member**: no administrative rights; participates in the Community's
shared history according to their own PrivacySettings; may leave the
Community voluntarily at any time.

---

## Invariants

- A Community has exactly one owner at any time. Unlike Match, GhostPlayer,
Playset, and Group, Community ownership **can** be transferred — a
Community is an ongoing, living thing whose leadership can legitimately
change hands, unlike a historical record or a personal roster.
- If an AdminGroup is set, it must be owned by the Community's current
owner.
- Transferring ownership clears the AdminGroup. The new owner must
explicitly designate their own Group as the new AdminGroup — an old
AdminGroup, owned by the previous owner, is never carried over.
- A User has at most one Membership per Community.
- Only the owner may change the AdminGroup itself; admins cannot grant
admin rights to anyone, including themselves.
- The owner cannot leave the Community or be removed from it — they must
transfer ownership first (see **Rights Model** for who may then leave).
- Removing a member who currently holds admin rights (a member of the
AdminGroup) requires the same authority as changing the AdminGroup
itself — only the owner. Any admin may remove a non-admin member.
- If a member who holds admin rights leaves voluntarily or is removed,
they are also removed from the AdminGroup as an automatic consequence —
a Community never holds an admin who is no longer a member. This is not
an exception to Group's "only the owner changes membership" rule: the
Community's owner and the AdminGroup's owner are always the same User,
so this is that owner's own Group being updated as a side effect of
their own Community, not a third party acting on it.

## Lifecycle

### Created

A Community is created by its (first) owner, with a Name, Description, and
JoinPolicy, and optionally an AdminGroup.

### Evolution

- The owner may transfer ownership (clearing the AdminGroup, per the
invariant above), set or change the AdminGroup, or change the
JoinPolicy.
- The owner or an admin may edit the Name and Description.
- Memberships accumulate as MembershipRequests are approved (or
immediately, if the JoinPolicy is `Open`).
- A member may leave voluntarily at any time, except the owner. An owner
or admin may remove another member, subject to the admin-removal rule
above.

### End State

A Community is deleted by its owner. Its Memberships end with it. Matches
already recorded that reference the Community remain valid historical
records regardless.

---

## Relationships

- Community is owned by exactly one User;
- Community optionally references an AdminGroup (a Group owned by the same
User as the Community's current owner);
- Community holds Memberships (0..N), one per member User;
- Community is targeted by MembershipRequests;
- Community is optionally referenced by Match.

---

## Domain Events

- CommunityOwnershipTransferred
- AdminGroupChanged
- MembershipAdded
- MembershipRemoved
- CommunityDeleted

Creation and Name/Description edits are not modeled as events, for the same
reason as Game and Group: nothing needs to react to them. Ownership,
admin rights, and membership changes are included because they directly
affect who can do what, and who can see what.

---

## Aggregate Design

Community is its own Aggregate Root. Membership is a child entity within
it — enforcing "at most one Membership per User" is a Community-scoped
rule, the same reasoning as MatchParticipant within Match. AdminGroup is
not owned by Community; it is a reference to a Group owned elsewhere,
exactly like Match's reference to Playset.
