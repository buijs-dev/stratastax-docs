## Goal
Remove a member from a Community you own or administer, without affecting
matches already recorded within it.

## Why
Communities sometimes need to remove a member — someone who no longer
belongs, or whose behavior warrants it. This should be possible without
rewriting history: matches already played remain part of the Community's
shared history regardless of who is a member today.

## Outcome
- The removed User is no longer a member of the Community.
- Any admin rights they held end at the same time.
- Matches already recorded within the Community remain unchanged, for
everyone.
- The removed User may request to join again later, subject to the
Community's JoinPolicy.
