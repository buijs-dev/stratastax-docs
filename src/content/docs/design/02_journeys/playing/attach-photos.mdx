## Goal
Add photos to a match record to preserve the moment alongside the
data — turning a score entry into a living memory.

## Why
A match is a social event. Photos capture what data cannot:
the table setup, the people, the moment someone won. Attached to a
match record, they become part of a player's Personal Gaming Archive — searchable,
tied to a specific game and group, and visible to the participants who were there.

## Outcome
- Photos are stored and linked to the match record.
- All participants in the match can view the photos, subject to Privacy Levels.
- Photos appear in your Personal Gaming Archive alongside the match data.
