## Goal
Claim a previously recorded Ghost Player and connect its complete gaming history to
your own Stratastax account.

## Why
Players should not lose years of gaming history simply because they created an account later. 
A match recorded before you joined Stratastax is still your match. 
Identity resolution exists to close that gap – retroactively and without affecting
anyone else in the same match.

## Outcome
- Historical matches become part of your archive.
- Statistics are recalculated to include all claimed history.
- Other participants in those matches remain unchanged.
- The Ghost Player becomes linked to the claimed Canonical Player Identity and is no longer treated as an independent participant.
