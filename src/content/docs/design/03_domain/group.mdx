## Purpose

A Group is a named collection of Users, managed by its owner, whose only
purpose is to be referenced by that owner's PrivacySettings — determining
who gets Privileged Access to the data types the owner chooses to share with
it. A Group has no meaning beyond being a roster: it does not itself grant
or restrict anything.

---

## Responsibilities

A Group is responsible for:

- having a Name;
- being owned by exactly one User;
- holding zero or more member Users, added or removed by its owner;
- being available for its owner's PrivacySettings to reference.

---

## Not Responsible For

A Group is NOT responsible for:

- deciding what data is visible to its members — that belongs entirely to
PrivacySetting; a Group by itself grants nothing;
- requiring consent from a User before they are added as a member — for
this model, being added to someone else's Group is unilateral, the same
way keeping someone in a personal address book requires no approval from
them;
- any approval workflow around membership — that does not exist for a
Group as modeled here (contrast with Membership Requests, used
elsewhere for Communities — see **Open Question**).

---

## Invariants

- A Group is owned by exactly one User, fixed at creation. Ownership is
never transferred.
- A Group's members are added or removed only by its owner.
- A PrivacySetting may only reference Groups owned by the same User as the
PrivacySetting itself — you can only grant access via your own Groups,
never someone else's (this resolves the open question left in
**PrivacySetting**).
- Removing a member from a Group takes effect immediately for every
PrivacySetting referencing it; there is no grace period.
- Deleting a Group removes it from every PrivacySetting that referenced it;
a Group is not "historical data" like a Match, so its deletion is not
blocked by being in use.

---

## Lifecycle

### Creation

A Group is created directly by its owner, with a Name and, optionally, an
initial set of members.

### Evolution

The owner may rename the Group, and add or remove members, at any time.

### End State

A Group is deleted by its owner at any time. Any PrivacySetting that
referenced it simply loses that grant.

---

## Relationships

- Group is owned by exactly one User;
- Group has zero or more member Users;
- Group is referenced by zero or more PrivacySettings (all owned by the
same User as the Group).

---

## Domain Events

- GroupMemberAdded
- GroupMemberRemoved
- GroupDeleted

Creation and renaming are not modeled as events — nothing needs to react to
a Group merely existing or being renamed, the same reasoning applied to
Game. Membership changes and deletion are included because they directly
change who can see what, which downstream access checks (and potentially
caches) legitimately need to react to.

---

## Aggregate Design

Group is its own Aggregate Root, not a child entity of User — deliberately,
even though every Group currently has a single owning User. The reason is
forward-looking: Group will very likely be reused by Community (see
**Open Question**), and keeping it independent avoids constraining that
design to User's aggregate boundary before Community itself is worked out.

---

## Resolved: Group and Community

Conceptually, "every Community has a Group at its core." Rather than
changing Group to support multiple owners or an approval-based membership
flow, Community (see **Community**) resolves this without touching Group at
all: a Community stores its own Memberships directly (via
**MembershipRequest**, its own approval-based mechanism), and separately,
optionally references an ordinary, single-owner Group as its **AdminGroup**
— reusing Group purely to delegate admin rights, not to store membership.
Group's shape and invariants above are unaffected by Community's
existence.
