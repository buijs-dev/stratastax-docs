## Goal
Share a Statistic Snapshot with a chosen audience — setting who can see it,
and actively drawing their attention to it.

## Why
Visibility and attention are not the same thing. A Snapshot's Privacy Level
determines who is allowed to see it, but a player may also want to actively
share it — for example by sending a link or a notification — so the intended
audience actually notices it, rather than relying on them to find it
themselves. Sharing complements Privacy Levels; it does not replace them.

## Outcome
- The Snapshot's Privacy Level determines who is allowed to see it.
- The Snapshot has been actively shared with the chosen audience.
- Other Snapshots and the underlying match records remain at their original visibility.
