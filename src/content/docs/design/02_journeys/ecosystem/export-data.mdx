## Goal
Download your complete gaming history in a portable, open format, so your
data is always yours, regardless of what platform you use in the future.

## Why
Data ownership is not meaningful without portability. Stratastax holds your
data on your behalf, it does not lock it in. You should be able to take your
full gaming history with you at any time, in a format that another platform
or your own tools can read.

## Outcome
- Your selected gaming history is available as a downloadable file.
- The export includes full match context: game, players, configuration, and results.
- The file is in an open format readable by other tools.
- No data is removed from Stratastax after export.
