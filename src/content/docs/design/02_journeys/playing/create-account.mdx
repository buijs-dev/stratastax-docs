## Goal
Create an account and establish your Canonical Player Identity.

## Why
Registration establishes everything required to participate in the Stratastax network.
It creates an account for authentication and a Canonical Player Identity that
becomes the permanent home for your gaming history.

## Outcome

Registration creates two distinct things:

* **An account** — the means by which you authenticate and access the platform.
* **A Canonical Player Identity** — the permanent player identity that your gaming history, statistics, and relationships are linked to.

These serve different purposes. The account enables access to Stratastax, while
the Canonical Player Identity represents you as a player within the network.

Once registration is complete, your Personal Gaming Archive is ready to receive gaming history.
Any gaming history previously recorded for you as a Ghost Player can now be claimed.
All future matches you participate in are automatically linked to your Canonical Player Identity.
