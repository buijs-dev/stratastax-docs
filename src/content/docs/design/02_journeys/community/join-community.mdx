## Goal
Become a member of a Community — such as a club, a regular playgroup, or a
tournament — and contribute your shared match history to it, without giving
up control over your own Privacy Levels.

## Why
Gaming communities have a shared history that outlives any single match.
When a player joins a Community, matches played within it start contributing
to a shared history and shared statistics — enriching the Community for
everyone, while each player keeps deciding what they personally share.

## Outcome
- Your Canonical Player Identity is a member of the Community's Group.
- Past matches where you appeared as a Ghost Player within the Community are surfaced for claiming.
- Future matches you play within the Community are automatically linked to your archive.
- The Community's shared history and statistics include only what you have chosen to share.
