This section documents the key user journeys in Stratastax. Each journey describes a user goal,
why it matters, the conceptual steps involved and the parts of the
[domain model](/design/03_domain) it touches.


## Playing

Recording what was played.

| Journey | Goal |
|---|---|
| [Attach Photos](/design/02_journeys/playing/attach-photos) | Add photos to a match record to preserve the moment alongside the data. |
| [Create Account](/design/02_journeys/playing/create-account) | Establish an account and a Canonical Player Identity on the Stratastax platform. |
| [Record Match](/design/02_journeys/playing/record-match) | Establish a match record in your Personal Gaming Archive. |
| [Record Playset](/design/02_journeys/playing/record-playset) | Create a reusable configuration of expansions, variants, and scenario for use in one or more matches. |

## Identity

Who a player is, and what they share.

| Journey | Goal |
|---|---|
| [Claim Ghost Player](/design/02_journeys/identity/claim-ghost-player) | Connect a previously recorded Ghost Player to your Stratastax account and recover its complete gaming history. |
| [Invite Friends](/design/02_journeys/identity/invite-friends) | Bring your regular gaming group onto Stratastax so shared history is connected. |
| [Manage Privacy Levels](/design/02_journeys/identity/manage-privacy) | Configure what data you share, with whom, and at what level of detail. |

## Community

Playing together in clubs and groups.

| Journey | Goal |
|---|---|
| [Generate Statistic](/design/02_journeys/community/generate-statistics) | Compute a fixed, point-in-time statistic from a specific scope within your Personal Gaming Archive. |
| [Join a Community](/design/02_journeys/community/join-community) | Become a member of a Community, such as a club, and connect your shared match history to it. |
| [Leave Community](/design/02_journeys/community/leave-community) | Voluntarily leave a Community you are a member of. |
| [Participate in Community](/design/02_journeys/community/participate-community) | Take part in your Community's ongoing activity — record matches within it, see shared rankings, and discover fellow members. |
| [Remove Community Member](/design/02_journeys/community/remove-community-member) | Remove another member from a Community you own or administer. |
| [Share Statistics](/design/02_journeys/community/share-statistics) | Share a Statistic Snapshot with a Group, publicly, or directly with someone. |

## Ecosystem

Your data in and out of Stratastax.

| Journey | Goal |
|---|---|
| [API Integration](/design/02_journeys/ecosystem/api-integration) | Connect a third-party application to the Stratastax platform via the open API. |
| [Export Data](/design/02_journeys/ecosystem/export-data) | Download your complete gaming history in a portable, open format. |
| [Import Data](/design/02_journeys/ecosystem/import-data) | Bring your existing gaming history from another tracker into your Stratastax archive. |
| [Revoke Application Access](/design/02_journeys/ecosystem/revoke-application-access) | Withdraw a third-party application's access to your data at any time. |
