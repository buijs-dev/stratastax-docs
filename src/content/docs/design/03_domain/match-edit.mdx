## Purpose

A MatchEdit represents a proposed change to a Finalized Match — a
correction to a MatchParticipant's result or Loadout, or a change to
which Playset a Match references (and with it, which expansions were
played). A Draft Match is not edited this way: its owner changes it
directly.
It exists because matches are entered by hand and mistakes happen.

The Match's owner (its original recorder) is the sole approver of any
MatchEdit. Other participants are never asked to approve — they are
notified instead, and retain their own way to flag a result they disagree
with (see **Dispute Flag**, on MatchParticipant).

A MatchEdit never mutates a Match directly. Like a ClaimRequest, it is a
proposal that must be approved before it takes effect, and its history
remains auditable even when rejected.

---

## Responsibilities

A MatchEdit is responsible for:

- proposing one or more changes to a Match's MatchParticipants or
Loadouts, or a change to which Playset the Match references;
- being approved or rejected solely by the Match's owner;
- respecting auto-approval where the owner has granted the proposer a
TrustedEditor relationship;
- notifying every CanonicalPlayer with a claimed MatchParticipant in the Match
once the edit is applied;
- preserving the original values for audit, whether approved or rejected.

---

## Not Responsible For

A MatchEdit is NOT responsible for:

- identity resolution or claiming — that belongs to ClaimRequest;
- resolving disagreement between participants — a participant's recourse is
a Dispute Flag on their own MatchParticipant, not a veto over the edit;
- changing a Draft Match — that is done directly by its owner, and a
MatchEdit on a Draft is refused (see **Finalized matches only**);
- editing a Match where edit requests have been disabled by its owner (see
**Edit Requests Toggle**) — in that case, only the owner may create a
MatchEdit at all.

---

## Who may propose, who approves

- **The Match's owner** may propose a MatchEdit on a Finalized Match at any
time, and approves their own proposals implicitly by creating them.
- **A participant** — any User whose CanonicalPlayer has a claimed
MatchParticipant in the Match — may propose a MatchEdit only if the Match's
owner has enabled edit requests for that Match. Such a proposal requires
the owner's approval before it takes effect, unless the owner has granted
that participant's User a TrustedEditor relationship, in which case it is
auto-approved.
- No one other than the owner is ever required to approve a MatchEdit.

---

## Notification

Once a MatchEdit is applied, every CanonicalPlayer with a claimed
MatchParticipant in the Match is notified of what changed. Notification is
informational only — it does not block or delay the edit, and it carries no
approval power.

---

## Edit Requests Toggle

Each Match's owner configures, per Match, whether other participants may
propose MatchEdits at all:

- **Enabled** (default): participants may propose edits, subject to the
owner's approval or an existing TrustedEditor grant.
- **Disabled**: only the owner may create MatchEdits for that Match.

---

## Finalized matches only

A MatchEdit targets a Finalized Match. While a Match is a Draft, its owner
changes it directly, so there is nothing to propose; a MatchEdit on a Draft
is refused. Finalized Matches can be corrected for as long as they exist:
there is no edit window.

---

## Invariants

- A MatchEdit targets exactly one Match.
- A MatchEdit is immutable once created; its lifecycle proceeds only
through approval events.
- A MatchEdit targets a Finalized Match, never a Draft.
- A MatchEdit proposed by a participant (not the owner) requires the
owner's approval, unless the owner has granted that participant's User a
TrustedEditor relationship.
- A MatchEdit proposed by a participant is refused outright if the Match's
Edit Requests Toggle is Disabled.
- Rejecting a MatchEdit never alters the Match; the original values stand.
- Applying a MatchEdit never deletes prior values — the previous state
remains visible in the Match's history.

---

## Lifecycle

### Proposed

Created by the Match's owner, or by a participant if edit requests are
enabled for that Match.

### Approved / Applied

The owner approves (explicitly, implicitly by being the proposer, or via an
existing TrustedEditor grant). The targeted MatchParticipants, Loadouts and/or
which Playset it references are updated; prior values remain part
of the audit trail. Affected
CanonicalPlayers are notified.

### Rejected

The owner declines. The Match is unchanged. No further process exists
today — a new MatchEdit may be proposed again later.

---

## Relationships

```
MatchEdit
├── targets         → Match (specific MatchParticipants / Loadouts, or
│                     which Playset it references)
├── proposedBy       → User
├── approvedBy       → Match owner (User)
├── autoApprovedVia  → TrustedEditor (where applicable)
├── notifies         → CanonicalPlayer(s) with a claimed MatchParticipant
```

## Domain Events

- MatchEditProposed
- MatchEditApproved
- MatchEditApplied

## Aggregate Design

MatchEdit is its own Aggregate Root, mirroring ClaimRequest: it coordinates
a single approval decision without owning the Match itself. Applying an
approved MatchEdit is a command against the Match aggregate, not something
MatchEdit performs directly.
