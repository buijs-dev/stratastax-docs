## Purpose

A TrustedEditor relationship lets a Match owner exempt a specific other User
from the approval step on MatchEdits — for example, "edits my brother
proposes to matches I own are automatically approved." It exists so that
people who trust each other completely do not need an explicit approval for
every small correction, without weakening the approval requirement for
everyone else.

Since a Match's owner is always its MatchEdit's sole approver, a
TrustedEditor grant only ever exempts that owner's own approval — it never
affects any other Match.

---

## Responsibilities

A TrustedEditor grant is responsible for:

- recording that one User (the grantor, acting as a Match owner) will
auto-approve MatchEdits proposed by another specific User (the trustee)
for matches the grantor owns.

---

## Not Responsible For

A TrustedEditor grant is NOT responsible for:

- granting any access to view data — that remains governed entirely by
Privacy Levels and Groups;
- editing Draft Matches — those are changed directly by their owner, and a
MatchEdit only targets a Finalized Match;
- reciprocity — granting trust to someone does not grant anything back.

---

## Invariants

- A TrustedEditor grant is directional: it names exactly one grantor and
exactly one trustee, and only exempts MatchEdit approval for matches the
grantor owns.
- A TrustedEditor grant never implies the reverse relationship.
- A TrustedEditor grant can be revoked by the grantor at any time; revoking
it has no effect on MatchEdits already applied.
- A TrustedEditor grant only applies to MatchEdits, so only to Finalized
Matches.

---

## Lifecycle

### Granted

A User grants trust to another specific User.

### Revoked

The grantor withdraws the grant at any time. From that point on, MatchEdits
from the former trustee follow the normal approval process again.

---

## Relationships

- TrustedEditor grant is issued by one User (grantor) to one User
(trustee).

---

## Domain Events

- TrustedEditorGranted
- TrustedEditorRevoked

---

## Aggregate Design

TrustedEditor is a child entity within the granting **User**'s aggregate —
it only ever changes as a consequence of that User's own decision, never as
a side effect of anything the trustee does.
