## Goal
Take part in your Community's ongoing activity — recording matches within
it, following its shared rankings, and discovering the players you share it
with.

## Why
Membership only becomes meaningful once it is lived. A Community's shared
history and statistics only grow through what its members choose to record
and share. Being active in a Community means contributing to that shared
picture, while always staying in control of what you personally reveal.

## Outcome
- Matches you choose to share become part of the Community's shared history.
- The Community's rankings and statistics reflect your contribution, where shared.
- You can see fellow members and, where they have chosen to share it, their activity.
- Nothing you keep private is exposed by your membership.
