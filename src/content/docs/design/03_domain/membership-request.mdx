## Purpose

A MembershipRequest represents a pending request to join a Community whose
JoinPolicy is `RequiresApproval`. It exists so that a Community's owner or
admins can control who becomes a member, without needing to reject anyone
by default — most requests are expected to be approved.

For a Community whose JoinPolicy is `Open`, no MembershipRequest is created
at all: a Membership is added directly.

---

## Responsibilities

A MembershipRequest is responsible for:

- proposing that one User becomes a member of one Community;
- being approved or rejected by the Community's owner or any of its
admins;
- resulting in a Membership once approved.

---

## Not Responsible For

A MembershipRequest is NOT responsible for:

- deciding the Community's JoinPolicy — that belongs to Community itself,
and determines whether a MembershipRequest is needed at all;
- granting any rights beyond membership — approval never makes someone an
admin; that is governed entirely by the Community's AdminGroup.

---

## Invariants

- A MembershipRequest targets exactly one Community and is proposed by
exactly one User.
- A MembershipRequest only exists for a Community whose JoinPolicy is
`RequiresApproval`.
- A MembershipRequest is immutable after creation; its lifecycle proceeds
only through approval events.
- A MembershipRequest may only be approved or rejected by the target
Community's owner or an admin (a member of its AdminGroup).
- A MembershipRequest cannot be created by a User who already has a
Membership in the target Community.
- Rejecting a MembershipRequest creates no Membership; the User remains
unable to record shared history within the Community.

---

## Lifecycle

### Pending

Created when a User requests to join a Community whose JoinPolicy is
`RequiresApproval`.

### Approved

An owner or admin approves the request. A Membership is created for the
requesting User.

### Rejected

An owner or admin declines the request. No Membership is created. A new
MembershipRequest may be submitted again later.

---

## Relationships

```
MembershipRequest
├── proposedBy → User
├── targets    → Community
├── decidedBy  → User (the Community's owner or an admin)
├── resultsIn  → Membership (if approved)
```

---

## Domain Events

- MembershipRequestCreated
- MembershipRequestApproved
- MembershipRequestRejected

---

## Aggregate Design

MembershipRequest is its own Aggregate Root, mirroring ClaimRequest and
MatchEdit: it coordinates a single approval decision without owning the
Community itself. Applying an approved request (creating the Membership) is
a command against the Community aggregate, not something MembershipRequest
performs directly.
