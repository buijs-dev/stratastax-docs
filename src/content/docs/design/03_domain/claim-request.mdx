## Purpose

A ClaimRequest represents a proposed relationship between a CanonicalPlayer
and one or more GhostPlayer references.

It does not modify identity directly. It introduces a pending edge that must
be validated before becoming authoritative.

In essence: *"This CanonicalPlayer believes these GhostPlayer references
belong to them."*

---

## Responsibilities

A ClaimRequest is responsible for:

- proposing ownership of one or more GhostPlayers;
- preserving intent and context of a claim;
- enforcing an approval workflow (anti-identity-theft protection);
- tracking approval/denial state;
- enabling reversible identity linking (via edge removal, not mutation);
- capturing who initiated and who validated the claim.

---

## Not Responsible For

A ClaimRequest is NOT responsible for:

- merging data into CanonicalPlayer;
- rewriting Match or Participation history;
- changing GhostPlayer records (beyond the ClaimEdge itself);
- computing statistics;
- resolving identity automatically;
- deciding correctness of identity.

Those belong to the Identity Resolution domain service and to Player graph
operations.

---

## Invariants

- A ClaimRequest always has exactly one claiming CanonicalPlayer.
- A ClaimRequest may reference one or many GhostPlayers.
- A ClaimRequest is immutable after creation (state changes only via
events).
- A GhostPlayer can be referenced by multiple ClaimRequests (conflict is
allowed).
- A ClaimRequest must be explicitly resolved: Approved or Rejected.
- Approval requires authorization from the GhostPlayer's owner (the User who
created it), unless pre-approved via a trusted invitation path.
- A ClaimRequest never deletes or alters GhostPlayer data.
- A ClaimRequest cannot directly change CanonicalPlayer state.

---

## Lifecycle

### Created

A ClaimRequest is created when:

- a user finds a GhostPlayer they believe represents them, OR
- the system suggests a match via identity resolution heuristics, OR
- an invitation-based pre-link is triggered.

State: `Pending`

### Under Review (optional intermediate state)

- awaiting confirmation from the GhostPlayer's owner;
- awaiting additional verification.

### Approved

When validated:

- a ClaimEdge is created;
- the GhostPlayer remains unchanged;
- the CanonicalPlayer gains a new relationship link.

State: `Approved`

### Rejected

When denied:

- no ClaimEdge is created;
- the request is stored for audit/history;
- future re-requests may be allowed depending on policy.

State: `Rejected`

### Revoked (optional future state, only reachable from Approved)

A previously approved claim can be reversed:

- the ClaimEdge is removed (not the data);
- the GhostPlayer remains intact;
- the CanonicalPlayer loses that association.

State: `Revoked`

---

## Relationships

```
ClaimRequest
├── originatesFrom → CanonicalPlayer
├── targets        → GhostPlayer(s)
├── validatedBy    → User (GhostPlayer owner / trusted invitation path)
├── resultsIn      → ClaimEdge(s)
```

---

## Domain Events

- ClaimRequestCreated
- ClaimRequestSubmitted
- ClaimRequestApproved
- ClaimRequestRejected
- ClaimRequestExpired
- ClaimRequestRevoked
- ClaimEdgeCreated
- ClaimEdgeRemoved

The real identity changes happen on the ClaimEdge events, not on the
request itself.

---

## Aggregate Design

ClaimRequest is an Aggregate Root, with a strict boundary: it only manages
request state, participants (claimer, targets, validator), and lifecycle
transitions. It does NOT own CanonicalPlayer or GhostPlayer.
