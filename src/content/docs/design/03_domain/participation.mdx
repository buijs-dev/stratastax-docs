## Purpose

A Participation represents one player's involvement in one specific Match:
who played (via a GhostPlayer reference), what they brought to the game
(their Loadout, if applicable), and what happened to them (their result).

A Participation is the only thing a Match ever references to represent a
player — never a GhostPlayer or CanonicalPlayer directly.

---

## Dispute Flag

A claimed Participation's CanonicalPlayer owner may mark it as **Disputed**,
with a reason. This is a visible annotation only — it does not block, alter,
or revert anything about the Match, and it does not trigger any resolution
process. It exists so a participant can register disagreement with a result
without needing anyone else's permission, in place of a formal dispute
mechanism.

A Dispute Flag can only be set by the User who owns the CanonicalPlayer
claiming that Participation's GhostPlayer — no one else can flag, or clear,
someone else's disagreement. The flag can be cleared by the same User at
any time.

---

## Responsibilities

A Participation is responsible for:

- referencing exactly one GhostPlayer;
- holding the player's result within the Match (score, rank, outcome);
- holding the player's Loadout, where the game defines one;
- allowing its claiming CanonicalPlayer's owner to set or clear a Dispute
Flag;
- remaining a stable, immutable historical record.

---

## Not Responsible For

A Participation is NOT responsible for:

- resolving or claiming identity — that belongs to ClaimRequest and
ClaimEdge;
- holding configuration shared across all participants (expansions,
variants, scenario) — that belongs to Playset, on the Match;
- computing statistics — Participations are read by statistics computation,
not responsible for it;
- knowing whether its GhostPlayer has been claimed.

---

## Invariants

- A Participation references exactly one GhostPlayer.
- A Participation belongs to exactly one Match.
- A Match has at most one Participation per GhostPlayer.
- Once its Match is Finalized, a Participation's result (score, rank,
outcome) does not change except through an approved MatchEdit (see
**MatchEdit**); while the Match is a Draft, its owner changes it directly. It is never silently
overwritten, and any prior value remains part of the audit trail.
- A Participation's Loadout, if present, follows the same rule.
- A Participation is unaffected by later changes to its GhostPlayer's
display name, or by any ClaimEdge created or removed afterward.
- A Dispute Flag can only be set or cleared by the User owning the
CanonicalPlayer that claims the Participation's GhostPlayer; an unclaimed
Participation cannot carry a Dispute Flag.
- Setting or clearing a Dispute Flag never changes the Participation's
result, Loadout, or any other data.

---

## Lifecycle

### Creation

A Participation is created when a Match is recorded, once for each
participant, alongside the Match itself.

### Evolution

A Participation's result or Loadout changes only through an approved
MatchEdit (see **MatchEdit**). Outside of an approved MatchEdit, nothing
about a Participation changes after creation.

### End State

A Participation is never deleted while its Match exists.

---

## Relationships

- Participation is referenced by Match;
- Participation references GhostPlayer;
- Participation holds Loadout.

---

## Domain Events

- ParticipationCreated
- ParticipationDisputed
- ParticipationDisputeCleared

---

## Aggregate Design

Participation is not its own Aggregate Root. It is a child entity within the
**Match** aggregate: Match enforces the consistency rules that involve all
of its Participations together (e.g. exactly one Participation per
GhostPlayer, a complete and coherent set of results).
