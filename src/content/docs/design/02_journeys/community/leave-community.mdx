## Goal
Leave a Community you no longer want to be part of, without affecting
matches you have already recorded within it.

## Why
Membership is never permanent. A player should be able to step back from a
Community at any time, without losing their own gaming history or
retroactively changing what already happened within that Community.

## Outcome
- You are no longer a member of the Community.
- Any admin rights you held end at the same time.
- Matches already recorded within the Community remain unchanged, for
everyone.
- You may request to join again later, subject to the Community's
JoinPolicy.
