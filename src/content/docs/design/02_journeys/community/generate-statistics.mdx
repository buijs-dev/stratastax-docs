## Goal
Compute a Statistic Snapshot for a specific scope of your gaming history —
a game, a Playset, a date range, an opponent, or a Group — as a fixed
result you can review and later share.

## Why
The Personal Gaming Archive continuously computes statistics across a
player's entire history, but a player often wants something narrower:
their win rate with one faction, their record in one Playset, their
history against one opponent. A Statistic Snapshot captures exactly
that scope, frozen at the moment it is generated, so it stays a
trustworthy, unchanging picture even as new matches are recorded later.

## Outcome
A Statistic Snapshot now exists in your Personal Gaming Archive, computed from
the scope you chose. It represents a fixed, point-in-time picture — it will not
change as new matches are recorded. The Snapshot is private by default, ready
to be shared later.
