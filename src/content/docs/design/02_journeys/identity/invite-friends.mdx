## Goal
Bring your regular gaming group onto Stratastax so that shared match history
becomes connected across both of your Canonical Player Identities.

## Why
The platform becomes more valuable when the people you play with are part of
the network. Matches you have already recorded together can be retroactively
linked once your friends join — their history does not start from zero,
it starts from the first match you recorded together.

## Outcome
- Your friend's Canonical Player Identity is linked to the Ghost Player in your matches.
- Shared match history appears in both archives.
- Statistics are recalculated for both players.
- The network grows: future matches between you are automatically linked.
