## Goal
Bring your existing gaming history from another tracker into your Stratastax
archive, without losing data or requiring manual re-entry.

## Why
Years of gaming history should not be held hostage by the platform it was
recorded on. Stratastax is designed to integrate rather than replace. If you
have been tracking games elsewhere, that history belongs to you and should
be portable into your Personal Gaming Archive, where it can grow further.

## Outcome
- Historical matches from another platform are part of your Personal Gaming Archive.
- Games are linked to the Stratastax catalogue where possible.
- Ghost Players are created for participants who are not yet on Stratastax.
- Statistics are recalculated to include the imported history.
