## Goal
Withdraw a third-party application's access to your data, at any time and
without needing the application's cooperation.

## Why
Ownership means you decide who has access, for as long as you choose. An
application only holds the access you granted it — that access is never
permanent, and taking it back should be as simple as granting it was.

## Outcome
- The application no longer has access to your data.
- Data previously written by the application remains in your Personal Gaming Archive.
- You can re-authorise the application again in the future, if you choose to.
