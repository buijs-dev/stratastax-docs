## Purpose

A Loadout captures what one player brought to one Match: their faction,
character, starting cards, or any other game-specific configuration that
applies to them individually, rather than to the Match as a whole.

Loadout is the per-player counterpart to Playset. Playset is shared and
applies equally to every participant (expansions, variants, scenario);
Loadout is individual and can differ from player to player within the same
Match.

---

## Responsibilities

A Loadout is responsible for:

- holding player-specific, game-defined configuration for one Participation;
- feeding faction- and character-level statistics (e.g. faction win rates).

---

## Not Responsible For

A Loadout is NOT responsible for:

- configuration shared across all participants — that belongs to Playset;
- the player's result (score, rank, outcome) — that belongs to
Participation directly;
- identity resolution.

---

## Invariants

- A Loadout belongs to exactly one Participation.
- A Loadout's fields are game-dependent and may be partially or fully empty
for games that do not define factions, characters, or comparable
concepts.
- A Loadout does not change except through an approved MatchEdit that
targets it — the same rule as Participation's result.

---

## Lifecycle

### Creation

A Loadout is created together with its Participation, when the Match is
recorded, for games that define player-specific configuration.

### Evolution

None. Immutable once created.

### End State

A Loadout is never deleted while its Participation exists.

---

## Relationships

- Loadout belongs to Participation.

---

## Aggregate Design

Loadout is a value object / child entity within the **Match** aggregate,
scoped under its Participation. It has no independent identity or lifecycle
outside of its Participation.
