## Goal
Configure how your gaming data is shared by assigning Privacy Levels to different types of data.

## Why
Your gaming data belongs to you. Different types of data deserve different
audiences, so Stratastax lets you configure Privacy Levels per
data type instead of applying a single account-wide setting.

## Outcome
- A default Privacy Level is configured.
- Your privacy settings are applied to all data you share.
- Future access to your data follows those rules.
- Other participants retain control over their own data.
- Your data contributes to anonymous, aggregated community statistics regardless of your individual Privacy Levels.
