This section describes Stratastax from the point of view of its users: why it exists, the ideas
it is built on, what people do with it, and the model behind it. It is the shared vocabulary of
the project; the API and the implementation follow from it.

| Section | Answers | Start with |
|---|---|---|
| [Introduction](/design/01_introduction) | **Why** Stratastax exists and **how** we think about identity, privacy and ownership | [Vision](/design/01_introduction/vision), [Concepts](/design/01_introduction/concepts) |
| [Journeys](/design/02_journeys) | **What** users do: their goals and the steps to reach them | [Record a match](/design/02_journeys/playing/record-match) |
| [Domain model](/design/03_domain) | **What** we define: aggregates, their rules and relationships | [Overview](/design/03_domain) |
| [Design system](/design/design-system/) | How Stratastax **looks**: colours, type, spacing and their rules | [Colours](/design/design-system/) |

Read them in this order: the journeys make sense once the concepts are clear, and the domain
model is what the journeys need. How it is implemented is in [Technical](/technical/).
